# Incubator Fit Review: ZENITH

## Verdict

**Strong fit.**

## Why it fits HACD

- ZENITH is *about* HACD. The product only makes sense because HACD names are a finite, structured namespace (6 letters from a 16-letter alphabet). It could not exist on any other chain.
- It is usage-first, not speculation-first: the ZEN token is the access key to a working engine, mirroring the proven model of the largest live Stack Asset on HACD.
- It deepens the HACD ecosystem rather than competing with it — it gives every HACD holder a reason to look up, score, and value the *name* they own.

## What HACD adds

- **PoW container:** each ZEN lot is formed on a real, mined HACD — the access right is backed by proof-of-work, not a database row.
- **Formation cost:** the 50 HAC stack cost per HACD is a visible, on-chain formation input, giving ZEN a transparent cost basis reference (not a price floor).
- **Asset history:** every ZEN lot carries a durable, publicly verifiable formation record on the Hacash explorer.
- **Community formation:** 224 public lots invite the HACD community to co-form the supply, aligning ZEN holders with the namespace they analyze.

## Why it is not a Carat clone

Carat Protocol scores HACD **market rarity** through HIP-5/8/9 — what a HACD is *worth*. ZENITH scores HACD **names** — what a HACD *says* (dictionary words, palindromes, repetition, rare letters). The two are complementary index layers, not competitors. ZENITH deliberately stays out of market-rarity scoring.

## Main concerns

- Utility must be demonstrably live at launch, not promised. (Addressed: the engine and API are already built and tested.)
- The namescore must be presented as an opinionated linguistic score, not a financial valuation. (Addressed in all copy.)
- Designated-first genesis requires the reserved address to be operated correctly. (Flagged for HACD Labs verification.)

## Required issuer confirmations

- Final stack cost (drafted at 50 HAC per HACD).
- Reserved address for the 32 first-phase lots.
- Launchpad enforcement of the 10-HACD-per-participant cap.

## Recommended next step

Confirm the reserved address and stack cost with HACD Labs, then proceed to Launchpad parameter review. The engine is deployable today.
