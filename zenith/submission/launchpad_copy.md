# ZENITH on HACD

## Hero

**Every HACD is a word. ZENITH tells you which words are worth owning.**

ZENITH issues ZEN — a usage-first Stack Asset that gates a live engine for scoring and discovering premium HACD names across the 16,777,216-name space.

## What is ZENITH?

There are 16,777,216 possible HACD names — six letters, drawn from exactly sixteen (`A B E H I K M N S T U V W X Y Z`). Most are noise. A rare few are real English words, palindromes, or single-letter "solid" names. ZENITH is the engine that finds them and scores them.

ZEN is the key to that engine. Hold ZEN, connect your address, and unlock the ability to search, score, and rank HACD names by an objective 0–100 namescore.

## Why HACD?

ZENITH could not exist anywhere else. The product is a direct consequence of HACD's defining rule: six letters from a sixteen-letter alphabet. That constraint is what makes the namespace finite and the best names ownable. Forming ZEN on HACD also backs each access right with real proof-of-work and a transparent, on-chain formation cost — the asset is formed, not merely deployed.

Bitcoin proved PoW for money. HACD brings PoW to assets. ZEN is formed through HACD.

## How Stack works

ZEN is formed across 256 HACD lots. Each lot is one HACD, pays a 50 HAC stack cost, and forms exactly 65,536 ZEN. When all 256 lots are formed, total supply is permanently fixed at 16,777,216 ZEN — one ZEN for every possible HACD name. No inflation. No hidden allocation.

Genesis is reserved-first: 32 reserved lots complete first for an auditable opening, then the remaining 224 public lots open to the community.

**Why these numbers:** 256 = 16² and 65,536 = 16⁴. Their product 16^6 = 16,777,216 equals the total HACD name space exactly. One ZEN exists for every possible HACD name. The supply structure is HACD-native by design.

## FAQ

**Do I need HACD?** Yes. Each Stack participation requires HACD according to the launch rules.

**Do I need HAC?** Yes. HAC is needed for the stack cost and network fees.

**Is this an investment product?** No. ZEN is a utility access token for the ZENITH engine. No profit, price, or return is implied.

**What does the namescore mean?** It is an opinionated linguistic and structural score, not a financial valuation. A high namescore means a name is rare or memorable as language — not that it has any market price.
