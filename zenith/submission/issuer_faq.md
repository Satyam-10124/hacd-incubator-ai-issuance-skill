# FAQ

## Do I need HACD?

Yes. Each Stack participation requires HACD according to the launch rules. ZEN is formed on HACD lots — one HACD per lot.

## Do I need HAC?

Yes. HAC is needed for the stack cost (50 HAC per HACD) and for network fees. Check the current network fee on the Hacash explorer before transacting.

## Is this an investment product?

No. This is a HACD Stack Asset launch. ZEN is a utility access token for the ZENITH naming engine. No profit, price, or return is guaranteed.

## What is the namescore?

It is an opinionated 0–100 score of a HACD name's linguistic and structural quality — whether it is a real word, a palindrome, has repeated or rare letters, and so on. It is not a valuation and not financial advice. A high namescore says a name is interesting as language, nothing about market price.

## How does ZENITH differ from Carat?

Carat Protocol scores HACD market rarity through HIP-5/8/9 — what a HACD is worth. ZENITH scores HACD names — what a HACD says. They are complementary. ZENITH does not produce market-rarity or price scores.

## What does holding ZEN unlock?

- Free: score any single name, check validity.
- Holder (≥ 1,000 ZEN): premium name search and the namescore leaderboard.
- Pro (≥ 10,000 ZEN): bulk scoring of up to 200 names and exports.

## Does ZENITH ever need my private key or seed phrase?

Never. Access is checked read-only against a public address balance. ZENITH does not ask for a private key, seed phrase, wallet password, keystore, or signature, and it never takes custody of funds.

## What happens if I remove my Stack?

Removing the Stack from a HACD lot burns the 65,536 ZEN tied to that lot and releases the HACD. The ZEN are permanently destroyed and the stack cost is not refunded.

## How long does formation take?

After you submit a Stack transaction, the Hacash mainnet typically confirms it within about five minutes, after which the formed ZEN appears on the Launchpad.
