# Project Profile: ZENITH

## One-liner

Every HACD is a 6-letter word — ZENITH is the engine that tells you which words are worth owning.

## Category

Utility (HACD-native naming / index layer)

## Problem

There are 16,777,216 possible HACD names, but no one has mapped which of them are special. A handful are real English words. A few are palindromes. Sixteen are a single letter repeated six times. The rest are noise. Today a HACD holder has no objective way to know whether the name they mined or bought is linguistically premium — or just another six letters.

## Asset concept

ZENITH issues ZEN, a usage-first Stack Token. ZEN is not a bet on a price; it is the key to the ZENITH naming engine. The engine scores any HACD name from 0–100 across five linguistic and structural dimensions (word, palindrome, repetition, letter rarity, structure) and lets holders search the premium subset of the namespace. Total supply is fixed at 16,777,216 ZEN — exactly one ZEN for every possible HACD name.

## Why HACD

ZENITH is impossible without HACD. The product is a direct function of HACD's defining constraint: 6 letters, drawn from exactly 16 (`A B E H I K M N S T U V W X Y Z`). That constraint is what makes the namespace finite and the premium names ownable. Forming ZEN on HACD also backs the access right with proof-of-work and a transparent on-chain formation cost.

## Target users

- HACD holders who want to know what their name is worth linguistically.
- Collectors hunting dictionary-word and palindrome HACDs before others notice.
- Builders and namers choosing a memorable HACD for a project or AI agent.
- The broader Hacash community, as a shared reference for HACD name quality.

## Launch readiness

The engine, scoring model, dataset (1,219 dictionary-word names indexed), API, and token-gating are already built and tested. Utility is live today, not roadmap. What remains is HACD Labs parameter review and the public Launchpad listing.
